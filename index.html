About

Research aligned with packaging, assurance, and inspection.

My current research direction aligns with the Security and Assurance (SCAN) Lab led by Dr. Navid Asadi at the University of Florida. The lab focuses on electronics inspection, metrology, failure analysis, assurance, and supply chain security, supported by advanced imaging and characterization infrastructure.

I am especially interested in advanced semiconductor packaging, physical attacks and inspection of electronics, and inspection-driven analysis of package and device structures. My earlier experience in electron ptychography, 4D-STEM, and quantitative characterization provides a strong foundation in imaging, data analysis, and semiconductor research.

Research

What I work on

My present focus is centered on semiconductor packaging, inspection, and electronics assurance, while building on a strong background in imaging and semiconductor characterization.

01

Advanced Packaging Inspection & Metrology

Research focused on advanced packaging structures such as chiplets, interposers, redistribution layers, and fine-pitch interconnects, where reliable inspection and metrology are essential.

  • Chiplets, interposers, and RDLs
  • X-ray and SAM based inspection
  • Reliability and metrology workflows
02

Physical Attacks & Inspection of Electronics

Exploring imaging and probing approaches for electronics inspection, including optical methods, SEM, X-ray, and related techniques used for failure analysis and trust assessment.

  • Reverse engineering awareness
  • Defect and anomaly inspection
  • Physical assurance of electronics
03

Hardware Assurance & Failure Analysis

Interest in physically grounded workflows for defect localization, counterfeit awareness, tamper assessment, and inspection-informed root-cause analysis of electronic systems.

  • Failure analysis perspectives
  • Counterfeit and tamper awareness
  • Security and trust focused inspection
04

Semiconductor Imaging & Characterization

My earlier work in electron ptychography, 4D-STEM, and semiconductor characterization continues to inform how I approach quantitative imaging and analysis problems.

  • Electron ptychography
  • 4D-STEM and quantitative analysis
  • Semiconductor interface characterization
