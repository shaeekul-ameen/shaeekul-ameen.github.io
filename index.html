About

Research shaped by imaging, physics, and computation.

My work centers on extracting quantitative structural information from nanoscale materials using advanced electron microscopy and computational reconstruction. At the Pico scale Engineering Research Lab (PERL), I develop and apply workflows for electron ptychography and atomic electron tomography to study atomic structure, lattice distortion, defects, and semiconductor interfaces.

Before joining the University of Florida, I taught electrical and electronic engineering courses across multiple universities in Bangladesh. That experience continues to shape how I communicate technical ideas, mentor students, and approach research problems with both rigor and clarity.

Research

What I work on

A focused research program connecting high-resolution microscopy with quantitative analysis of semiconductor structure and behavior.

01

Electron Ptychography & 4D-STEM

Phase retrieval and multislice reconstruction for atomic-resolution imaging, with computational workflows built around PtyRAD, abTEM, HyperSpy, and Python.

  • Diffraction-data phase retrieval
  • Multislice reconstruction
  • Atomic-column localization
02

Atomic Electron Tomography

Three-dimensional atomic reconstruction from tilt-series projections for structural characterization of nanoscale materials and defects.

  • 3D atomic reconstruction
  • Tilt-series analysis
  • Nanoscale defect characterization
03

Semiconductor Strain & Interface Analysis

Quantitative lattice-spacing and strain analysis of semiconductor nanostructures, including Si/SiGe interfaces and local lattice distortions.

  • Gaussian peak fitting
  • Lattice and strain mapping
  • Interface characterization
04

Nanoelectronics & Device Physics

Earlier research focused on carbon-nanotube field-effect transistors, numerical device modeling, and the influence of geometric parameters on transport behavior.

  • CNTFET simulation
  • Device parameter analysis
  • VLSI and nanoelectronics
